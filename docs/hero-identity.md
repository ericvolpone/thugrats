# Hero identity (built; this is the design it follows)

**The problem:** every kit has a gun or projectile, a melee option, an aura, a summon, an AOE and a nuke, so the heroes blur together. Some abilities never get picked because they don't feel like that hero.

**The idea:** give each hero **one signature mechanic**, trim the generic pieces, and replace them with abilities that feed the mechanic. The pairing talents and gear sets already push in this direction.

## Lenny "Nickles": the Boss (money and crew)
- **Signature:** gold is power. Abilities scale gently with the gold you're holding, and Hired Goon, Drive-By and Hit Contract are "calling in favours".
- **Replace:** Molotov and Pineapple, which overlap Boom.
- **Add:**
  - *Protection Racket*: thugs inside an aura pay out gold and take more damage.
  - *Made Men*: call two goons at once.
- **Rework:** Tantrum becomes *Make an Example*, a fear nova.

## Tiny Tony Two-Fists: the Brawler (no guns, ever)
- **Signature:** a **Rage** meter that fills from taking and dealing hits. Knocking a thug back into a wall deals slam damage.
- **Replace:** Manhole Cover, which is a projectile.
- **Add:** *Headlock*: grab a thug and throw it into the others.

## Boom-Boom Bianchi: the Fuse
- **Signature:** everything is delayed and chains. Explosions set off other explosives, and fire spreads.
- **Merge:** fold Gas Can into Napalm.
- **Add:** *Detonator*: sets off every placed bomb at once.

## Lucy "The Ghost": the Assassin (stealth, poison, execute)
- **Signature:** **Marks** stack from poison and traps, and executes cash them in.
- **Replace:** Silenced Pistol, a gun that overlaps Babyface.
- **Add:** *Shadow Step*, a blink-strike.
- **Rework:** Shuriken Ring applies marks instead of being a generic aura.

## Babyface Moretti: the Professional (few huge hits)
- **Signature:** **Focus**. Fewer, slower, guaranteed-crit shots, with weak spots on big thugs.
- **Remove:** Claymore and Car Bomb (explosives that overlap Boom) and Rain of Lead.
- **Add:** *Spotter* (marks show weak spots) and *Double Tap*.

## Professor Pudding: the Mad Scientist
- **Signature:** **status combos**. Freeze, shock, acid and shrink combine; for example shock plus freeze shatters and acid plus fire is corrosive.
- **Rework:** abilities become ingredients, and summons are the Prof's machines.

## Result
Each hero ends up with about 10 abilities that only make sense on them. Build it one hero at a time, keeping the talent pairings and gear sets aligned with the new kit.
